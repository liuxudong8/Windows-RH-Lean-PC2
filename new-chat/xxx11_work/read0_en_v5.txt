# 2　Variational Mechanism and Real-Axis Convention

Lamzouri (arXiv:2609.02882v2), Proposition 2.1: for a conjugation-invariant multiset Z, η∈L², supp η⊂(−λ,λ), η²^(0)=1, K=η²^ (entire, K(0)=1), the number of simple real zeros is ≥ 2Σ1 − ΣK(z−s)² — online proportion ≥ 2−A, A=ΣK(z−s)²/Σ1 (unconditional; no termwise nonnegativity of K required). The constant C_MT=1/2+(1/√2)cot(1/√2)=1.3274992963 is the optimal (minimal) pair-correlation constant in this method family: Lemma 3.2 constructs the optimal kernel η_ε (f₀(x)=cos(√2x)/(√2sin(1/√2)), support [−1/2,1/2]), pair constant C=Q(0)+2∫₀¹αQ(α)dα (Q=f*f); Remark 3.4 (Carneiro–Chandee–Littmann–Milinovich) confirms C_η≥C_MT — within the unconditional framework no kernel constant in the family of spectral support λ≤1/2 can fall below 1.3275.

Real-axis confirmation: Z_T={i(ρ−1/2)logT/2π} gives z=−γ·logT/2π∈ℝ for ρ=1/2+iγ — the pair sum ΣK(z−s)² of Proposition 2.1 is computed on the real axis (K even). Spectral support λ is the variational degree of freedom: the unconditional case (the BGS theorem requires f of support [−1,1]) pins λ≤1/2; relaxing λ requires the pseudorandom (GUE) premise — precisely the “pseudorandom version of the Montgomery–Taylor variational problem”.

# 4　Decomposition Argument and Union Online Proportion

The zeros of the union Z_K=Z_ζ∪Z_L are mutually distinct (cross-family common values =0, verified numerically) — N₀ˢ_K=N₀ˢ_ζ+N₀ˢ_L. Decomposition argument: apply Proposition 2.1 to the two families separately (kernel family of spectral support λ≤1/2, constant C_MT), then weight by zero counts — union online proportion ≥ (N_ζ(2−A_ζ)+N_L(2−A_L))/N_K. Under the pseudorandom premise (audit6 optimal kernel): (999354×0.9921+1149836×0.9886)/2149190 ≈ 0.990 ≥ 0.875=7/8 — the union meets the threshold. Cross-family pairings (interleaving, no repulsion; audit5 A_cross_extra≈0.55–1.48) are completely bypassed in the decomposition argument — the essential distinction between the direct union argument (A_union=1.29–1.62, online proportion 0.377–0.714) and the decomposition argument.

# 6　Pseudorandom Union Theorem (Conditional Form)

Theorem (weak-premise pseudorandom union theorem, conditional form). Assume the following two explicit hypotheses: (H1) there exists a kernel K₁ (within the audit6 optimal-kernel family) such that the pair-correlation constant of the zeros of ζ on the normalized scale satisfies A_ζ(K₁)≤1.125; (H2) the single-family pair correlation of L(s,χ₅) satisfies a BGS-type explicit formula (i.e., the unconditional BGS pair-correlation theorem generalized to the fixed real character χ₅ — the BGS-type theoretical coordinate in which the L-family pair constant A_L is anchored and controllable), and there exists a kernel K₂ such that A_L(K₂)≤1.125 (numerical convention: audit6 optimal kernel fejer_a0.5, A_ζ=1.0079, A_L=1.0114). Then the union online simple-zero proportion is ≥ (N_ζ(2−A_ζ(K₁))+N_L(2−A_L(K₂)))/N_K ≥ 7/8.

Proof skeleton: (i) N₀ˢ_K=N₀ˢ_ζ+N₀ˢ_L (zeros distinct, verified); (ii) apply Proposition 2.1 separately: N₀ˢ_ζ≥2N_ζ−ΣK²_ζ, N₀ˢ_L≥2N_L−ΣK²_L (unconditional inequalities, K=η²^, K(0)=1); (iii) by definition A_ζ=ΣK²_ζ/N_ζ and A_L=ΣK²_L/N_L (A_L controlled in the BGS-type coordinate of (H2)); Proposition 2.1 then gives N₀ˢ_ζ≥(2−A_ζ)N_ζ and N₀ˢ_L≥(2−A_L)N_L; (iv) substitute the premises (H1)–(H2) (A_ζ, A_L≤1.125) and weight. The key point of the decomposition argument: cross-family pairings never enter (in contrast with the failing direct union argument, A_union=1.29–1.62).

# 7　Honest Boundaries

① The measured A is data (T=6×10⁵), not a theorem — the premise A≤1.125 is unproved; the theorem is purely conditional. ② No unconditional version of the L-family pair-correlation formula exists in the literature (Özlük needs GRH; Yıldırım needs GRH + cross-character average) — the BGS generalization to a fixed-modulus real character is an open construction within the framework, so the theorem states it as the explicit hypothesis (H2) (the L family satisfies a BGS-type formula), separated from the numerical premise A_L≤1.125: without (H2), A_L≤1.125 is merely numerical data, with no BGS-type theoretical coordinate (the controlled pair constant is undefined). ③ The band-test z-values are not used for precise verdicts because of the normalization-constant effect (order-of-magnitude support only). ④ The kernel-family scan is finite (fejer/sinc/gauss/MT families), not exhausting the full variational solution space; the trivial limit A→1 as a→0 is identified, but the optimal reading is restricted to the nearest-neighbor scale. ⑤ Finite-T effects of the δ scale and the R truncation are partially calibrated (subinterval convergence); extrapolation in T requires larger data. ⑥ The weights N_ζ/N_K=0.465, N_L/N_K=0.535 in the union decomposition come from the T=6×10⁵ measurement.
